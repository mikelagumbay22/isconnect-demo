Whether your goal is to study, build a career, or eventually settle long-term, International Students Connect Inc. is here to guide you every step of the way.

At International Students Connect Inc., we understand that planning a move to New Zealand can feel like a big step — but you’re not doing it alone. We’ve designed a smooth, step-by-step process to guide you from your first inquiry all the way to settling in this beautiful, forward-thinking country.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Consultation & Goal Setting

  2. 2

    Step 2: Pathway Recommendation

    Based on your profile, we’ll recommend the most suitable programs and visa options available in New Zealand. Whether it’s university, vocational training, or a skilled work route, we’ll help you move forward with clarity.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Document Preparation

    We’ll assist you in gathering the necessary documents, making sure everything meets New Zealand’s immigration and academic requirements — all while keeping the process simple and organized

  4. 4

    Step 4: Application Filing

    Once everything is ready, we’ll take care of preparing and submitting your applications — for both your study program and your visa — with accuracy and attention to detail.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Pre-Departure Guidance

    Before you fly, we’ll help you get ready for your new life in New Zealand — offering practical tips on travel, housing, and what to expect when you arrive.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Post-Arrival Assistance - Welcome to New Zealand!

    Landing in a new country is just the beginning. We’ll continue to support you as you settle in, ensuring you feel confident and connected from day one.
