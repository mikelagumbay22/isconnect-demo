Ireland

Your Academic & Career Gateway to Ireland

Ireland has quickly emerged as a top choice for international students and young professionals — and for good reason. With globally respected universities, a booming job market, and a warm, inclusive culture, Ireland offers a unique environment where you can grow academically, professionally, and personally.

At International Students Connect Inc., we’re here to help you make the most of what Ireland has to offer — from choosing the right study program to exploring opportunities that lead to long-term success.

Ireland

What We Offer

01

Study Program Guidance

We help you identify and apply to the best-fit universities or colleges in Ireland — ensuring your chosen course supports both your academic background and future ambitions.

02

Student Visa (Stamp 2)

Our team supports you through the full visa process — from gathering required documents to ensuring compliance with INIS (Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service) guidelines.

03

Post-Study Work Opportunities

Under the Third-Level Graduate Scheme, you can stay in Ireland for up to two years after graduation. Your chosen school or Institution will guide you through the application so you can gain real-world experience and explore career prospects.

Ireland

Ireland Application Process

At International Students Connect Inc., we take the guesswork out of planning your studies in Ireland. Our process is designed to be clear, supportive, and fully tailored to your goals — from your first inquiry to your first day on campus, and beyond.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Initial Consultation

    We begin with a personalized consultation to understand your academic background, goals, and long-term plans. We also assess your eligibility for study programs and visa requirements under Irish immigration rules.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Program & Pathway Planning

    Based on your profile, we recommend suitable Irish universities or colleges, guide you in course selection, and explain your options for staying in Ireland after graduation.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Document Preparation

    Our team assists in compiling all required documents, including:

    • Proof of acceptance from an Irish institution
    • Proof of finances (minimum funds required by INIS)
    • Passport and identity documents
    • Proof of medical insurance
    • Other supporting documents as needed We ensure all documents meet INIS (Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service) standards.
  4. 4

    Student Visa Application (Stamp 2)

    Once your documents are ready, we guide you through the application for the Irish Study Visa including completion of the online application (AVATS), visa fee payment, and embassy submission where required.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Pre-Departure Guidance

    Before you fly to Ireland, we help you prepare with travel advice, accommodation tips, and cultural insights. We’ll also brief you on your rights and responsibilities as a student visa holder.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Post-Arrival Assistance - Welcome to Ireland!

    After you arrive, we continue to support you to helping you settle into your new academic and social life.

At International Students Connect Inc., we’re committed to helping you not just study abroad, but thrive abroad. With expert guidance and personalized support, we make your move to Ireland smooth, secure, and full of possibility.
