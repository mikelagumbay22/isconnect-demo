International Students Connect

Who we are

International Students Connect Consultancy Inc. (ISConnect) is a leading international education consultancy helping students achieve their academic and career goals abroad. We provide expert guidance on study abroad programs across top destinations, including Canada, the UK, Ireland, Germany, Spain, New Zealand, Australia, the USA, and select Asian countries.

Founded in Canada through our parent company, Alma Armada & Company Ltd., we began by partnering with reputable Canadian institutions to support students with university applications, program selection, student visa assistance, and admissions processing. Our strong results in Canada enabled us to expand globally, establishing partnerships across Europe, the US, Australia, and New Zealand.

Our team of experienced education consultants delivers personalized, end-to-end support throughout the study abroad journey—from choosing programs to securing admissions and visas. With ISConnect, students access world-class study opportunities and build pathways for a successful future abroad.

Our Growth Story

01

Humble Beginnings

We began as a small team of passionate international education consultants committed to helping students access high-quality study abroad opportunities. From day one, our mission was to guide students toward the right academic programs and global career pathways.

02

Building a Strong Foundation

We established a comprehensive student recruitment and study abroad agency offering personalized support for university applications, program selection, and student visa processing. Our services were designed to provide end-to-end guidance for international students pursuing education overseas.

03

Remarkable Growth

Within just two years, we expanded our reach and supported hundreds of students with customized study abroad solutions across Canada, Europe, the UK, Australia, and Asia. By combining expert counseling with streamlined admissions support, we helped students secure placement at reputable universities worldwide.

04

Today's Success

We are now recognized as a trusted study abroad consultancy and global education partner, collaborating with leading schools and universities to expand international learning opportunities. Our student-first approach, proven track record, and global network enable us to deliver high-value education consulting services that empower students to succeed abroad.
